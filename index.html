Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

All games and apps listed on this site are developed by Zoho Games. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This policy applies to every game and app we publish on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personally identifiable information from users. However, our apps may collect limited information automatically for functionality and advertising purposes, including:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version)
  • Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob and Unity Ads for showing ads)

2. How We Use Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game experience
  • Display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and Unity Ads
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

You can learn more about their privacy policies here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Updates take effect as soon as they are posted on this page.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not host user data. For any data collected by third-party SDKs, users can manage their privacy via device settings (Reset Advertising ID). For any queries, contact us at the email below.
