Privacy Policy for Block Blast King!

Developer: Zoho Games

Effective date: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Zoho Games ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Block Blast King! (the "Game").

2. Information We Handle

The Game is designed to save gameplay information such as scores, progress, stars, sound preferences, difficulty settings, and daily visit information locally on your device.

Based on our current review of the project's listed dependencies and Android configuration files, we did not find the common advertising or analytics SDKs searched for. We have not independently verified every part of the compiled application.

We do not operate a user account system or a game server for collecting gameplay information, based on the current implementation details available to us.

3. Local Storage and Data Retention

Gameplay preferences and progress may be stored locally on your device using browser storage. This information generally remains until you clear the Game's app data, clear the relevant storage, or uninstall the Game. The exact behavior can depend on your device and operating system.

4. Advertising and Analytics

We did not find the common advertising or analytics SDKs searched for during our initial project review. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain the applicable information practices.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intentionally transmit locally stored gameplay information to our own servers. Your device, operating system, app distribution platform, or other services outside our control may process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a Game-operated account or server. If the Game's features or third-party services change, we will review this policy and our practices accordingly.

7. Security

Keeping gameplay information on your device reduces the need to transmit that information to us. However, no storage method or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can generally remove locally stored gameplay information by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game. This may permanently delete your saved progress.
